What Is the Health & Social Care Career & Qualification Finder?

The Health & Social Care Career & Qualification Finder helps you explore suitable health and social care qualifications based on your career goals, previous experience and desired level of progression.

Use the finder to explore qualification levels, typical entry requirements, study duration, potential career options and progression pathways. The recommendations are designed to help you identify qualification options that may suit your career goals.

How to Use the Health & Social Care Career Finder

The finder asks a series of questions about your career goals, experience and qualification preferences. Your answers are then used to help identify suitable health and social care qualification options.

  1. Choose your main health and social care career goal.
  2. Select your current level of experience or qualifications.
  3. Choose the qualification level you are interested in.
  4. Review the qualification options and potential career pathways.

Health & Social Care Qualification Levels

Health and social care qualifications are available at different levels, allowing learners to develop their knowledge and progress towards different career responsibilities.

Level 2 Health & Social Care Qualifications

Level 2 qualifications can provide a foundation for learners who are starting their career in care and support or developing their understanding of health and social care.

Level 3 Health & Social Care Qualifications

Level 3 qualifications can help learners develop their knowledge and skills and progress towards senior care and support responsibilities.

Level 4 Health & Social Care Qualifications

Level 4 qualifications can support further professional development and progression towards supervisory, coordination and related responsibilities.

Level 5 Health & Social Care Qualifications

Level 5 qualifications can support learners progressing towards senior, supervisory and management responsibilities in health and social care.

Level 6 Health & Social Care Qualifications

Level 6 qualifications can support advanced professional and management development for learners looking to progress within the health and social care sector.

Level 7 Health & Social Care Qualifications

Level 7 qualifications can support advanced leadership and management development, depending on the qualification and the learner's career pathway.

Health & Social Care Career Progression

Health and social care qualifications can support career progression from entry-level care and support roles towards senior, supervisory, management and leadership responsibilities.

A typical progression pathway may involve developing knowledge and experience at one qualification level before progressing to a higher level. However, the most suitable pathway depends on your previous qualifications, experience, career goals and the requirements of your chosen role.

What Qualification Do I Need to Work in Health & Social Care?

The qualification you need depends on the specific health or social care role, your previous experience and the requirements of the employer. Some learners begin with an entry-level qualification, while others may already have relevant qualifications or experience that allow them to consider higher-level study.

If you are unsure which qualification level is suitable for your career goal, use the Career & Qualification Finder above to explore your options.

How to Choose a Health & Social Care Qualification

Choosing the right health and social care qualification depends on several factors, including your current qualifications, experience, career goals and the level of responsibility you want to achieve.

  • Career goal: Consider the type of health or social care role you want to progress towards.
  • Current qualifications: Check whether your existing qualifications meet the entry requirements.
  • Experience: Consider your previous professional or sector experience.
  • Qualification level: Choose a level that aligns with your current position and intended progression.
  • Study requirements: Check the duration, assessment methods and study format before choosing a qualification.

Can I Study a Health & Social Care Qualification Online?

Yes. Some health and social care qualifications can be studied through online or distance learning. The available study options depend on the qualification, awarding organisation and course provider.

Before enrolling, check the qualification requirements, study format, assessment method and awarding organisation to make sure the course is appropriate for your intended career pathway.

Frequently Asked Questions About Health & Social Care Qualifications

What qualification do I need to work in health and social care?

The qualification you need depends on the specific health or social care role, your existing qualifications and experience, and the requirements of the employer. Entry-level roles may require a lower qualification level, while senior and management roles may require higher-level qualifications.

What is the best qualification for a career in social care?

The best qualification depends on your career goal, current qualifications and experience. Level 2 and Level 3 qualifications can support entry and progression in care roles, while higher levels can support supervisory, management and leadership pathways.

Can I study a health and social care qualification online?

Yes. Some health and social care qualifications are available through online or distance learning. Before enrolling, check the qualification requirements, assessment method, study format and awarding organisation.

What is the difference between Level 2 and Level 3 health and social care?

Level 2 generally provides foundational knowledge and skills for care and support roles, while Level 3 can provide more advanced knowledge and support progression towards senior care and support responsibilities.

What can I do with a Level 5 health and social care qualification?

A Level 5 health and social care qualification can support progression towards senior, supervisory and management responsibilities, depending on the qualification, employer and individual's experience.
